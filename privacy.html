Retail Hell Privacy Policy

Effective 30 October 2026

The short version

Retail Hell does not collect, send or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What the game stores

The game saves two things on your own device so they are there next time you play:

  • your best shift score
  • whether sound is on or off

These stay on your device. They are never sent to the developer or anyone else. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

Internet and permissions

The game files load once from the game's website and are then kept on your device so the game works offline. The app asks for no special permissions, such as location, camera, microphone or contacts.

Children

Retail Hell is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer using the email address on the Retail Hell page in the Google Play Store.
