4D Check Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

4D Check is an app that shows 4D lottery results. It is made by Money (Yu Qing Chiew), the developer named on the app's Google Play listing. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it.

Information we collect

None. 4D Check has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, store or share your name, contact details, location, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Information kept on your device

The app saves a few things in your phone's own storage so it works the way you set it up and can open without internet:

  • which operators you show or hide, and the order you put them in
  • the last results it downloaded
  • the last screen you used

This stays on your device and is never sent to us. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it.

Results service

To show results, the app downloads them from 4dlivetoday.com, a free public results service. When you look up a number, that number is sent so the service can return its history. Like any website, that service receives your IP address as part of the request. No other information about you is sent. See 4dlivetoday.com for how it handles requests.

Gambling

4D Check only shows published results and number history. It does not sell tickets, take bets, process payments or link to betting. It is not affiliated with Magnum, Sports Toto, Da Ma Cai or any other lottery operator.

Age

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over, and it is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
