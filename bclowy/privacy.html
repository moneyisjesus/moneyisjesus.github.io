B.C.LOWY 隱私政策

生效日期：2026年10月31日

B.C.LOWY 應用程式顯示 bclowychinese.blogspot.com 部落格上的文章，由 Money（Yu Qing Chiew）製作。

我們收集的資料

沒有。本應用程式沒有帳號、登入、廣告或分析工具，不會收集、儲存或分享您的個人資料。

儲存在您手機上的資料

您的收藏、字體大小和上次載入的文章只儲存在您的手機上，方便離線閱讀，不會傳送給我們。清除應用程式資料或解除安裝即會刪除。

文章來源

文章從 Google 的 Blogger 平台載入。與任何網站一樣，Google 會收到您的 IP 位址等連線資料，請參閱 Google 隱私權政策。文章中的外部連結會在瀏覽器中開啟，並受該網站的政策約束。

Privacy Policy

Effective 31 October 2026

The B.C.LOWY app shows posts from the blog bclowychinese.blogspot.com. It is made by Money (Yu Qing Chiew).

Information we collect

None. The app has no accounts, sign-in, ads or analytics, and does not collect, store or share personal information.

Information kept on your device

Your saved posts, text size and the last posts loaded are stored only on your phone so the app works offline. They are never sent to us, and clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes them.

Where posts come from

Posts are loaded from Google's Blogger platform, which receives connection data such as your IP address, as with any website. See the Google Privacy Policy. Links inside posts open in your browser and are covered by those sites' own policies.
